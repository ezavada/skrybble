# Asset rights

Skrybble currently ships no third-party visual or audio assets. Its UI and
drawings are generated by the application code. User-created `.skrbl` files
are data, are ignored by Git, and are not covered by the project's MIT
license unless their author says otherwise.

The vendored PDG MVC source is covered by `vendor/PDG-LICENSE`. The downloaded
PDG runtime and its third-party notices remain covered by the licenses
included in the verified PDG release archive.
